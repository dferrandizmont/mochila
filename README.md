# Mochila

Mochila turns school messages into the family agenda. Copy a message from the
class group and paste it into Mochila, or give it a photo or a PDF of a school
letter, a lunch menu or a timetable. It picks out what to bring, who picks up,
the activities and the payments, each on its own day, and you review everything
before it is added. The app never goes into your WhatsApp groups: it only reads
what you copy.

## Free during the launch

During the launch, Mochila is free and shows no ads. You can start without
creating an account: without one, your family can run up to 3 analyses a day
and 20 a month (each notice Mochila reads counts as one analysis). If you link a
free Google or Apple account, you can invite the other adult to share the
agenda, go up to 20 analyses a day and 200 a month per family, and keep access
if you change phones.

## What it does

- **Today**: what each child takes, what is on at school and what is due that
  day. In the afternoon it already shows the next day, and every evening a
  summary reminds you what tomorrow holds: what to bring, pay and sign.
- A **home screen widget** shows what to bring today and tomorrow without
  opening the app.
- Mochila **suggests** the date, the time, the child and what to bring, pay or
  sign; you confirm, edit or discard each item. If you like, it goes into your
  phone's calendar too.
- **As a family**: invite the other adult with a code and you both see the same
  things, dates, tasks, the bag and who picks up. Each child has their own colour.
- Your region's **official school calendar** and your town's local holidays are
  already there, and you can add your school's own days off.
- Each child's **timetable and lunch menu**, the after-school activities and the
  school payments by child and term, which you can export as CSV.
- **Five languages**: Catalan, Spanish, English, Basque and Galician. Light and
  dark mode, and five colour ranges.

## Privacy

Photos and PDFs are read on your phone and only the text is sent; if you choose
a more accurate reading of a timetable, a menu or a school calendar, the image
is sent for that reading only and is not stored. Before anything leaves the
phone, phone numbers and e-mail addresses are replaced with placeholders, and
the full text of a message is not kept on the server: only what you confirm
and, unless you turn it off, a short quote. For your children, only a first
name or nickname, school and class are stored, never surnames.

## Is it open source?

No. The app is free to use, but its code is not public: it needs a server that
reads the messages. This repository holds information, release notes and the
issue tracker.
